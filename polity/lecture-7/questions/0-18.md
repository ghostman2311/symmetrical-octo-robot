
## 4. Mains Angle

**Likely question:** Explain the procedure for amending the Indian Constitution under Article 368. How does it reflect the balance between flexibility and rigidity?

**Four-line answer skeleton:**
1. Article 368 empowers Parliament to amend the Constitution through addition, variation or repeal.
2. A Bill may be introduced in either House and must satisfy the prescribed special majority in each House.
3. The President is constitutionally required to give assent after the Bill is duly passed.
4. State ratification for specified amendments provides a federal safeguard while allowing Parliament substantial amending power.
