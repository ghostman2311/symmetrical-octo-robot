## Self-Test

1. Give four reasons why the Indian Constitution is lengthy.
2. Why is Article 40 important to the Gandhian vision?
3. Why are DPSPs described as non-justiciable?
4. How does the Constitution balance religious freedom with social reform?
5. Does the Constitution declare Hindi to be India’s national language?
6. What did Ambedkar mean by the “Grammar of Anarchy”?
7. Why did Ambedkar warn against hero-worship in politics?
8. Explain the difference between political democracy and social democracy.
9. What is constitutional morality?
10. Which amendments gave constitutional status to Panchayats and Municipalities?

## Answer Key

1. Borrowed experience, detailed administration, precision, India’s diversity, one constitutional framework, and influence of the Government of India Act, 1935.
2. It directs the state to organise village panchayats.
3. Article 37 says courts cannot enforce DPSPs directly, although they are fundamental in governance.
4. Articles 25–26 protect religious freedom, while constitutional provisions allow regulation and reform within legal limits.
5. No. Article 343 identifies Hindi in Devanagari script as the Union’s official language; the Constitution declares no national language.
6. Normalising extra-constitutional methods when constitutional routes are available.
7. Hero-worship can weaken accountability and threaten democratic institutions.
8. Political democracy concerns political equality, including equal voting rights; social democracy requires liberty, equality and fraternity in social life.
9. A cultivated commitment to constitutional values and the spirit in which institutions should function.
10. The 73rd Amendment (Panchayats) and 74th Amendment (Municipalities).
