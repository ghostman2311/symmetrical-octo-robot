## Self-Test

1. What is meant by “intelligent adaptation”?
2. Which part of the Indian Constitution contains Fundamental Rights?
3. Why does the US right to bear arms illustrate selective borrowing?
4. Give two arguments against calling the Constitution a slavish imitation of the West.
5. Who is associated with the phrase “lawyer's paradise”?
6. State four reasons why the Indian Constitution was originally so detailed.

### Answer Key

1. Selecting and modifying ideas from other constitutions to fit Indian conditions rather than copying them blindly.
2. Part III.
3. India did not adopt an equivalent fundamental right to bear arms.
4. The Assembly adapted borrowed provisions; constitutional principles had been debated during the freedom struggle; and the Constitution's democratic and social-welfare aims differed from colonial rule. (Any two.)
5. Sir Ivor Jennings.
6. Accumulated constitutional experience, detailed administration, India's diversity, Centre–State relations, a single constitutional framework and the aim of reducing ambiguity. (Any four.)
