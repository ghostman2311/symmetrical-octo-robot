## Self-Test Questions

1. What is meant by the criticism that the Indian Constitution was “borrowed”?
2. Why is the Government of India Act, 1935 important to the Indian Constitution?
3. Name two constitutional features associated with the Government of India Act, 1935.
4. Which Article of the Indian Constitution concerns cow protection?
5. From which country was the idea of Directive Principles of State Policy borrowed?
6. Give two arguments against describing the Constitution as a carbon copy of foreign constitutions.

## Answer Key

1. The criticism is that many provisions were taken from foreign constitutions or earlier colonial legislation.
2. It supplied an important administrative and constitutional framework that influenced several provisions of the Constitution.
3. Examples include the office of Governor, Public Service Commissions, and elements of federal and administrative arrangements.
4. Article 48.
5. Ireland.
6. The Assembly debated and adapted provisions to Indian conditions, and the Constitution established its own framework of democratic government, rights and constitutional accountability.
