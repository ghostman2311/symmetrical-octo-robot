## Practice Questions

1. How many Parts and Schedules did the Constitution originally contain?
2. Which amendment introduced Part IVA?
3. Which amendment introduced Part IXA?
4. Which Part was repealed by the 7th Amendment Act, 1956?
5. Which country inspired India's parliamentary system?
6. What is the First-Past-the-Post system?
7. Which Article empowers the Supreme Court to issue writs?
8. Under which Article can High Courts issue writs for purposes beyond Fundamental Rights?

### Answer Key

1. 22 Parts and 8 Schedules.
2. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976.
3. 74th Amendment Act, 1992.
4. Part VII.
5. United Kingdom.
6. The candidate receiving the highest number of votes wins.
7. Article 32.
8. Article 226.
