## Practice Questions

1. What is the defining feature of a republic?
2. Which Article guarantees equality of opportunity in public employment?
3. What does popular sovereignty mean?
4. Which three types of emergencies are provided in the Indian Constitution?
5. Under which Article is President's Rule imposed?
6. Which Fundamental Rights are protected from suspension under Article 359?
7. Which constitutional features were borrowed from Australia?
8. What is the more accurate term for the British Constitution?
9. Why is the difference between written and unwritten constitutions considered one of degree?

## Answer Key

1. The head of state is elected, not hereditary.
2. Article 16.
3. Ultimate political authority originates from the people.
4. National, State/Constitutional and Financial Emergencies.
5. Article 356.
6. Articles 20 and 21.
7. Concurrent List, joint sitting of Parliament, and freedom of trade and commerce.
8. Uncodified Constitution.
9. Because codified constitutions also contain conventions, while uncodified constitutions contain written laws and judicial decisions.
